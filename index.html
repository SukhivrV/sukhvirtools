Sukhvir Tools

A Revit add-in that automates structural workflows so you can focus on design.

The Problem

Revit is powerful, but structural production work is full of repetitive, error-prone tasks - numbering piles, columns, beams, and walls, splitting elements at levels, joining geometry, and creating sheets from Excel. These tasks eat hours every week and slow teams down.

The Solution

Sukhvir Tools adds a dedicated ribbon tab to Revit with 13 tools designed for real structural workflows. No scripting. No Dynamo graphs. Just click and done.

What It Does

Automates Numbering

Number piles, columns, beams, and walls intelligently - detecting stacks, rows, and orientation to assign sequential marks automatically.

Splits Elements

Split columns and walls at any intermediate level with a single click, creating properly segmented elements with copied marks.

Streamlines Documentation

Batch-create sheets from Excel, assign views to sheets, and fix annotation overlaps - all from the ribbon.

Simplifies Coordination

Join geometry between selected categories, apply steel coping automatically, and extract pile coordinates with ease.

Compatibility

Sukhvir Tools supports Autodesk Revit 2025.
