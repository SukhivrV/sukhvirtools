Why StructiFlow?

Revit is powerful, but structural production work is full of repetitive, error-prone tasks that eat hours every week. StructiFlow removes the busywork—no scripting, no Dynamo graphs, just click and done.

Automates Numbering

Number piles, columns, beams, and walls intelligently—detecting stacks, rows, and orientation to assign sequential marks automatically.

Splits Elements

Split columns and walls at any intermediate level with a single click, creating properly segmented elements with copied marks.

Streamlines Documentation

Batch-create sheets from Excel, assign views to sheets, and fix annotation overlaps—all from the ribbon.

Simplifies Coordination

Join geometry between selected categories, apply steel coping automatically, and extract pile coordinates with ease.

Compatibility

One installer. One tab. Ready to work in your production models today.
