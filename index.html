Why StructiFlow?

Revit is powerful, but structural production work is full of repetitive, error-prone tasks. StructiFlow turns those tasks into simple, repeatable workflows.

Automates Numbering

Number piles, columns, beams, and walls intelligently—detecting stacks, rows, and orientation to assign sequential marks.

Explore numbering →

Splits Elements

Split columns and walls at an intermediate level with a single click, creating properly segmented elements.

Explore splitting →

Streamlines Documentation

Batch-create sheets from Excel, assign views to sheets, and fix annotation overlaps from the ribbon.

Explore documentation →

Simplifies Coordination

Join geometry, apply steel coping, and extract pile coordinates without leaving your Revit workflow.
